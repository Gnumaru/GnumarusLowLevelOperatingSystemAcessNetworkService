Email Client Showcase

Read-only IMAP inbox over the low-level socket proxy (WebSocket transport preferred, HTTP polling fallback). Sign in with a password (LOGIN) or with Google OAuth2 (XOAUTH2, handled by the local service); either way the inbox loads as soon as you are in. Listing and opening messages only — sending, deleting or flagging are not implemented by design.
